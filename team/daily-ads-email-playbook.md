# Daily-Ads Email Playbook

From Monte Horton, for the people on my Daily-Ads team.

**How to use this:** upload this file to ChatGPT (or Claude), paste the prompt from https://montehorton.net/team/#prompt, and ask for an email on any hook below. Read what comes back, put your own story and your own link in it, and send it. One email a day is the habit.

**Ground rules for every email:**

- One idea, one link. Don't stack three offers in one email.
- Tell the truth. No income claims, no "I made $X", no screenshots of money, no promises about what someone will earn. Talk about what the tool does and what you do with it.
- Write like you talk. Short lines. A little story beats a list of features.
- Always remind new people to click the confirmation email Daily-Ads sends them. Their account isn't active until they do.
- Use your own Daily-Ads referral link with a source tag (Affiliate Toolbox → type a source name), so you can see which emails bring sign-ups.

## Email shapes that work

**The story email:** a moment from your week → the lesson in it → one Daily-Ads action → one link → sign-off. P.S. with a reply invitation.

**The tip email:** the mistake → why it costs clicks → three short steps → one link.

**The question email:** one question → why you're asking → "hit reply" → nothing else.

**The feature email:** what the feature is → who it's for → the 2-minute way to try it → one link.

Subject lines: lowercase and conversational works well ("one question for you", "the ad every member sees first"). Keep them under about 50 characters.
