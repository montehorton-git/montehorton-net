How it works

The follow-up plan

Getting someone to sign up is the start. Following up is where the team gets built.

  1. Send the welcome email to every person who joins through you, the same day. Not just the ones who upgrade.
  2. Email them every day for the first 30 days, or at least every other day. One idea and one link each time.
  3. After 30 days, keep going. Daily is best; weekly at the very least. People join when they're ready, sometimes months later.
  4. Put it in an autoresponder so it runs without you. The tools I use are on my tools page.
  5. Stay honest. No income claims, no screenshots of money, no promises. Talk about what Daily-Ads does and how you use it.

Five minutes

Your daily routine

  1. Log in to Daily-Ads and view ads for about five minutes. That keeps your own ads in rotation.
  2. Check one number: which of your ads got a click yesterday? Keep that one running.
  3. Refresh your Daily-Swipes ad if it has drifted down the board.
  4. Send one email to your list. Pick a swipe below, or a hook from the playbook.
  5. Answer any replies. That's where the relationships start.

The playbook and the prompt

The playbook has 42 hooks, the Daily-Ads facts you can rely on, and the email shapes that work. The prompt turns any hook into a short, honest email.

  1. Download the playbook below.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Claude and upload the playbook.
  3. Paste the prompt, fill in the brackets, and pick a hook.
  4. Read the draft, put a true detail from your own week in it, and send.

You are helping me write one short follow-up email to the people who joined Daily-Ads through my link. I've uploaded my Daily-Ads Email Playbook. Use only the facts in it.

Write the email on this hook: [PASTE A HOOK FROM THE PLAYBOOK]

My details:
- My name: [YOUR FIRST NAME]
- My Daily-Ads referral link: [YOUR LINK, WITH A SOURCE TAG]
- A true detail from my week or my life I'd like to open with (optional): [ONE OR TWO SENTENCES]

Rules:
1. 120 to 200 words. Short paragraphs, one to three sentences each. Plain text, no headings, no bullet symbols except a numbered list of up to three steps.
2. Friendly and personal, like a note from someone who uses Daily-Ads every day. Open with "Hey there {name}," or "Hi {name},".
3. One idea and one call to action. Put my link on its own line after a short phrase, like: "Log in and try it: [link]".
4. Only use facts from the playbook's "Facts you can use" list. If I gave a personal detail, use it as I wrote it and don't add to it. Never invent a story, a number, a member, or a result.
5. No income claims of any kind: no dollar amounts earned, no "make money", no promises about results. Prices of plans are fine.
6. If the email is about joining or getting started, include one line reminding them to click the confirmation email Daily-Ads sends.
7. End with a short sign-off and my name. Add a one-line P.S. that invites a reply.
8. Give me three subject line options under 50 characters, lowercase and conversational.

Then check your draft against rules 3, 4 and 5 and fix anything that breaks them before you show it to me.
